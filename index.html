Booking & cancellation terms
  • The rate per plate depends on the sessions and menu you choose. It is shown, including tax, once your menu is complete.
  • Extra dishes in some sections, costly cookies, extra snacks and special items are charged extra per plate. All charges are shown in your quotation.
  • Hall charges of ₹3,000 are added once to the total amount payable, however many sessions are booked.
  • To confirm the booking, pay an advance of 75% of the total amount. The full amount must be paid before the event.
  • Cancellation cannot be done in the last 3 days before the date of the event.
  • If the booking is cancelled, only 25% of the total amount is payable.
